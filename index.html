How it works

  1. Create an account

    Sign up with a username and email. It takes under a minute.

    Open the dashboard and check which machines are online.

  3. Drive

    Use the arrow keys, WASD, a gamepad or the on-screen buttons. Each session is limited to 5 minutes.

About Us

Remote Drive is a student project. The aim is to let anyone drive a small robot over the internet, safely and with a clear queue.

This first version is the website only. The next stage is connecting an ESP32, a camera and a four-wheel robot.

What Is This

You control a real machine from far away. You see through its camera and send driving commands with your keyboard, a gamepad or your phone.

Right now

The driving screen is a simulation. Nothing you press controls real hardware.

Later

A backend server will handle secure login, the queue, live video and the connection to the robot. Safety controls such as an emergency stop and an automatic timeout will be added first.
